Your EcoFlow battery, in your Mac's menu bar. Real time remaining, automatic protection, 100% local over Bluetooth.

Download for Mac

macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon · Free & open source

🔋

Real time remaining

Battery %, corrected time-to-empty and power draw next to your clock — like a MacBook, powered by your River or Delta.

🛡️

Tiered protection

Notification, low power mode, then a clean shutdown that saves your session and restores it at next login. Every threshold is yours.

♥️

Battery health

Write charge and discharge limits straight to the battery — 80–85% daily keeps LFP cells young. Displayed % follows the usable window.

📈

24 h history

Battery level, wall draw and internal CPU power on one chart, with daily energy counters.

📡

100% local

Direct Bluetooth LE to the battery. No cloud, no polling servers, works fully offline — ideal for vans and off-grid setups.

🌍

Five languages

English, Français, Deutsch, 日本語, 中文 — follows your system, switchable anytime.
